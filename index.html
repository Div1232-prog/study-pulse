A SIMPLE STUDY ROUTINE

About StudyPulse

StudyPulse is a free student focus timer, Pomodoro study tracker for exams, and browser-based productivity tool for students. Plan a task, focus for one session, and review your progress without creating an account.

How can I focus while studying for exams?

Choose one specific task, set a manageable focus period, and remove distractions before you begin. StudyPulse uses a simple 25-minute focus session followed by an optional five-minute break, helping you work in clear, repeatable blocks.

What is the best free Pomodoro timer app for students?

The best timer is one that is easy to start and fits your study routine. StudyPulse is a free, no-account Pomodoro study tracker with task priorities, focus history, streaks, and gentle break reminders. Your progress stays in your browser.

Can I use StudyPulse as a free offline study planner?

Yes. StudyPulse stores tasks, preferences, and session history locally in your browser. When served over HTTPS or localhost, its optional Progressive Web App cache can also keep the app shell available offline after the first successful visit.
